Eighteen short chapters, written to be read on a phone away from the guitar. Each covers one topic in short sections, with diagrams, and ends with a few things to take to the guitar and a handful of questions to check yourself.

The badge on each chapter is the practice level where it first matters. Read a little ahead of where you're playing. Chapters 1–8 are the foundation — especially {{link:learn:changing-chords | Chapter 8, on changing chords}}, which names the drills the practice packets use.
