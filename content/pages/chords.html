Every chord shape the program uses, grouped by type. Movable shapes are stored at one representative root — E-shapes (root on string 6) mostly at G, fret 3; A-shapes (root on string 5) mostly at C, fret 3. Slide them so the square lands on the root you need; {{link:learn:movable-shapes | Movable shapes}} and {{link:learn:fretboard-map | The fretboard map}} explain how. Symbols you don't recognise are decoded in {{link:learn:chord-name-decoder | the chord-name decoder}}.
